About the Department

The Department of Computer Engineering at Vishwabharati Academy is dedicated to shaping future leaders in technology. With a focus on cutting-edge innovations, we prepare students for the dynamic world of software development, AI, and emerging technologies.

Academic Programs

We offer the following programs:

  • Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Computer Engineering: A comprehensive four-year undergraduate program covering programming, algorithms, data structures, and software engineering.
  • Master of Engineering (M.E.) in Computer Engineering: A two-year postgraduate program focusing on research in areas such as machine learning, cybersecurity, and IoT.
  • Diploma in Computer Engineering: A three-year foundational program designed to introduce students to core computing concepts.

Why Choose Us?

  • Industry-oriented curriculum designed in collaboration with top IT companies.
  • Live projects, hackathons, and workshops to gain hands-on experience.
  • Strong placement assistance with leading MNCs and startups.
  • Advanced research labs and innovation centers.

Facilities

Our department offers state-of-the-art infrastructure, including:

  • Fully-equipped computer labs with high-performance systems.
  • Specialized AI, cloud computing, and networking laboratories.
  • Library with 10,000+ technical books and research journals.
  • 24/7 access to online learning platforms and coding environments.

Faculty

Our faculty comprises experienced professionals with expertise in AI, software development, cybersecurity, and more. They are committed to mentoring students and driving innovative research.
